About

WHERE TRANCE
BECOMES
TRANSCENDENCE

Brenny and the Tiger are an American electronic music duo from Los Angeles — masters of sonic architecture who fuse trance subgenres into something completely their own.

Their music doesn't just pulse — it breathes. Airy melodies collide with intense basslines. Upbeat synths wrap around catchy rhythms. Every track tells a story and invites you to feel something real.

They don't settle for formulas. They push into new aural territory with each release, building soundscapes that capture the senses and refuse to let go.
